# Geopolitical & Macro Transmission Strategy

## Objective
Exploit asymmetric macro and geopolitical dislocations (crude oil supply shocks, currency devaluations, tariffs, and interest rate divergences) using the systemic causal chain.

## Target Universe
- Commodity-sensitive Indian equities (Oil & Gas, Paints, Tyres, IT Exporters, Metal producers).

## Transmission Chain Protocol
1. **Trigger Event**: Brent Crude surges > 8% in 5 trading sessions due to geopolitical escalation.
2. **First-Order Shock**: India import bill expands, USD/INR weakens, inflation expectations rise.
3. **Sector Propagation**:
   - Upstream Beneficiaries: ONGC, Oil India (higher crude realization).
   - Downstream Victims: Asian Paints, Berger Paints, Apollo Tyres, InterGlobe Aviation (margin squeeze).
4. **Trade Execution**:
   - Long Setup: Buy ONGC on breakout of 20-day high with volume surge.
   - Short/Hedge Setup: Short or avoid input-cost sensitive equities failing 50 SMA support.

## Risk Rules
- Position size capped at 6% of portfolio equity per trade.
- Mandatory Stop Loss: 4% trailing stop.

## Invalidation
- Diplomatic de-escalation or sudden crude drop > 5% terminates the trade thesis.

## Time Horizon
- 1 to 4 Weeks.

## Required Data
- Brent Crude futures (`BZ=F`), USD/INR spot (`INR=X`), Daily equity OHLCV.
