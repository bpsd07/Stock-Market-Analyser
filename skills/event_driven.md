# Event-Driven & Corporate Catalyst Strategy

## Objective
Capitalize on substantial market mispricings triggered by corporate announcements (mega order wins, PLI scheme approvals, large capex announcements, demergers, and buybacks).

## Target Universe
- All NSE listed equities with market cap > ₹1,000 Crore.

## Entry Conditions
1. Catalyst Verified: Regulatory filing on NSE/BSE detailing an order win exceeding 25% of annual revenue or major strategic corporate action.
2. Price Confirmation: Stock opens with a gap-up < 4% or breaks out of intraday consolidation with at least 3x average volume.
3. Fundamental Accretion: Order or action directly expands forward EBITDA margins or ROCE.

## Confirmation
- Delivery percentage > 60% on exchange data (indicating long-term institutional accumulation rather than day-trader churn).

## Risk Rules
- Max capital allocation: 8% of portfolio equity.
- Stop Loss: Low of the event-announcement candle.

## Exit Rules
- Take profit at 15% and 25% price appreciation.
- Exit on receipt of adverse regulatory clarification.

## Invalidation
- Immediate exit if management issues dilutive equity or guidance revisions lower margins.

## Time Horizon
- 1 to 3 Months.

## Required Data
- Exchange corporate filings, delivery volume, quarterly financials.
