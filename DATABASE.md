# Database Architecture & Schemas

## 1. Dual-Engine Architecture
The database layer uses **SQLAlchemy 2.0 Async ORM** configured for seamless dual-mode execution:
1. **Development & Laptop Mode**: Zero-configuration local SQLite (`data/trading_platform.db`) utilizing `aiosqlite`.
2. **Production Mode**: High-throughput PostgreSQL with TimescaleDB extension utilizing `asyncpg`.

## 3. Decision Traceability & Audit Logs
Every trading recommendation generated by the multi-agent system stores an immutable `decision_records` row containing:
- Exact raw indicators at moment of calculation.
- The 14 analyst outputs (including the Bear Analyst critique).
- Model and prompt version.
- Traceability SHA-256 hash.

This allows complete historical accountability: "Why was Tata Motors recommended on October 8th?" can be answered with the exact evidence and adversarial counterarguments recorded at that timestamp.
